Why TechNav

Tech is huge. Your class starts in five minutes.

Room numbers like AG26 or A170C don't tell you which wing, which floor, or which staircase. TechNav does.

First week, new building

New students, visitors and anyone with a class in an unfamiliar wing can type a room number and get a route, no asking around.

Step-free when you need it

Turn on the step-free option and every floor change uses an elevator. Useful with a mobility aids, crutches, a cart or a heavy bag.

Across floors, not just hallways

Routes go between floors, from the basement to Floor 4, and show the right floor plan for each step as you go.

How it works

Three steps from lost to there.

1

Choose From and To

Start typing a room number or place name, like Main Entrance, and pick it from the list. Tick "Step-free route" if you want elevators only.

2

Follow the route

Each step shows one instruction with the route drawn on that floor's plan. Tap Next to move on; the floor plan changes when you do.

3

See every step at once

Tap "View all steps" for the whole route as a list, and jump back to any step if you lose your place.

What's inside

More than a map.

Room search and route map

Search any room, then follow step-by-step instructions with the route drawn on the floor plan.

Hallway photos

Where available, see real photos taken at fixed points along your route, so you know what the hallway looks like.

Report issues

Flag a wrong route, correct a room's entrance, or contribute a photo. Submissions are reviewed before anything is published.

How to get it

Nothing to install. Just open the link.

TechNav is a website, so it works on iPhone, Android and laptops alike.

technav-db7a8.web.app

  2. 2Optional: use your browser's Share → Add to Home Screen so it sits next to your apps.
  3. 3Pick your start and destination, then tap Get Directions.

Privacy

What's collected, and who sees it.

Using the app: analytics

No account and no GPS location. Firebase Analytics records aggregate usage counts on the live site, using a random device ID, not your name. Hosting, database and sign-in run on Google Firebase.

Reports and photos

Reports save your text, floor, map pin and route context (start, destination, step). Photos are resized to 1600 px, re-encoded as JPEG and stripped of metadata. Authorized reviewers approve them; approved photos and entrances are visible to everyone.

Feedback: what you type

Read only by the TechNav team and the CS 392 teaching staff. Email is optional. Nothing is sold or shared with other third parties.

PROTOTYPE

Built by students, as a class project

TechNav was built by students for CS 392 at Northwestern in Fall 2026. It's a working prototype, not an official university service, and it's here so real people can try it and tell us what works.

  • It may stop working after the quarter. There's no promise the site stays online or gets updates once the course ends in December 2026.
  • Directions can be wrong. Floor plans and rooms may be imprecise and distances are estimates. If a route looks off, trust the signs and tell us through the feedback form above.
  • Not for emergencies. In an emergency, follow posted exit signs and building staff, not this app.
  • Not affiliated with Northwestern facilities. Room data may be out of date.
