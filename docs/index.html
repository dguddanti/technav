Prototype notice

TechNav is a prototype built for Northwestern CS392. It may contain bugs. Maps and photos may be incomplete. It may not be maintained past the quarter.

01

What it is

  • Room search with step-by-step instructions and a route map.
  • Hallway photos at fixed points along the route, where available.
  • Issue reporting: wrong routes, entrance corrections, and photo contributions.
  • Submissions are reviewed before anything is published.
02

Who it is for

Anyone navigating the Northwestern University Technological Institute (Tech). The building has split levels and long hallways that are difficult to navigate on a first visit.

The app runs in a mobile browser. It is free. Nothing to install.

03

How to use it

  1. Open technav-db7a8.web.app on a phone.
  2. Search for a destination room and pick a starting point.
  3. Follow the steps. Use the report button to flag errors.

Scan to open the app on your phone.
Source code: github.com/dguddanti/technav.

04

Data collection

The app collects:

  • Submitted reports: text, floor, map pin, and route context (start, destination, step).
  • Submitted photos: resized to 1600 px max, re-encoded as JPEG, original metadata removed.
  • Aggregate usage counts via Firebase Analytics on the production site.

Authorized reviewers read reports to approve them. Approved photos and entrances become visible to all app users. Hosting, database, and authentication run on Google Firebase. Nothing is sold or shared with other third parties.
